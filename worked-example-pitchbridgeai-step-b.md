  AI's venture profile, the six candidate countries, and the seven required
  screening criteria at the weights the founders carried forward from their

- `runs/2026-08-31-*` — this first run's filled-in evidence table (48

## Task 3 — Opportunity interpretation (labeled AI interpretation)

Canada and Spain rank highest mainly because they score well on buying
power, infrastructure, economic freedom, and country risk — criteria where
wealthy, stable economies structurally outscore emerging markets. India
ranks third almost entirely on the strength of its market-size and
consumption-capacity scores, both driven by sheer population rather than
buying power or ease of doing business, where it scores near the bottom.
Brazil, Indonesia, and Mexico cluster lowest because none of them leads on
any single criterion the way Canada, Spain, or India do.

## Task 4 — Risks/unknowns (why founders should not take this ranking at face value)

- **Weak proxy — market size & growth:** total national population is a
  generic proxy, not a PitchBridge-specific one; PitchBridge sells to
  founders and small-business owners, not to the general population, so
  raw population overweights India's score.
- **Weak proxy — consumption capacity:** "estimated internet users" is an
  AI-derived figure (population x internet-use rate), not an independently
  published statistic, and it duplicates the infrastructure criterion
  rather than measuring a distinct concept like digital-consumer spending.
- **Partial proxy — country risk:** only the World Bank's Political
  Stability component was used; it omits inflation/FX volatility, rule of
  law, and regulatory uncertainty.
- **Dated data:** India's internet-use rate is from 2022.
- **Projection, not outturn:** GDP figures are April-2026 IMF projections.
- **No paywalled library-database evidence yet:** this run used only
  public/open sources; no Statista, Euromonitor Passport, IBISWorld,
  MarketLine, or Business Source Complete data has been incorporated.

## Task 5 — Recommended next action

Before this scorecard is used in Step C: (1) replace or supplement the
population-based market-size indicator with a venture-specific proxy —
SME/startup counts, accelerator/incubator density, or a
serviceable-available-market estimate; (2) broaden country risk with at
least one additional governance or macro-stability indicator; (3) pull at
least one paywalled library-database data point per shortlisted country to
reduce reliance on generic macro indicators alone.
