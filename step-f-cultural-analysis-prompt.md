# Step F — Cultural Analysis & Comparative Table Prompt

**IBA 250: Building a Born-Global Digital Firm — Assignment 2, Step F**

This is the prompt template your team will use to complete Parts C and D of
Assignment 2 ("Cultural analysis," Section 3.4, and the required
"Cross-cultural comparison table," Section 3.5, of the project guide). This
step assumes you've already done **Step D** (so you know which countries)
and **Step E** (so you have launch-relevant findings to connect cultural
evidence to). Fill in the bracketed placeholders, paste the whole block into
the AI tool of your choice, and keep the raw output — it becomes Section 5 of
your report.

## Culture is a lens, not a stereotype

The project guide is explicit about this, and it's worth reading before you
paste the prompt in: "Use cultural frameworks as analytical lenses, not as
stereotypes. Country-level tendencies do not describe every individual
customer." A Hofstede score or a high-/low-context classification is a
hypothesis about a population's central tendency, not a fact about any one
customer you'll actually talk to. The prompt below instructs the AI to treat
it that way — don't let your final report strip that caution back out.

## After you run it

1. Save the raw output and log it in the **AI Use & Founder Decision Log** —
   verify at least one cultural-dimension score yourself against the
   original source (e.g., the Hofstede Insights site) before it goes in your
   report.
2. Drop Tasks 1-2 into **Section 5 (Cultural Environment)** of your report,
   including the required comparative cultural attributes table.
3. Drop Task 3 into **Section 6 (Launch Implications)**.
4. Write **Section 7 (AI Use, Verification, and Founder Judgment)**: name at
   least one thing the AI proposed that your team verified, challenged, or
   changed after reviewing the evidence — the closing sentence from Task 3
   above is a good place to start.
5. Now that Steps D, E, and F are all done, go back and finish **Section 1
   (Executive Decision Summary)** — state the selected countries, entry
   mode, and the three most important reasons behind the decision, drawing
   on all three steps.
6. Publish the six required Stage 2 social-media videos (2 per founder,
   faces visible) on your team's account from Assignment 1, and include the
   six links in your report. Content ideas: why you selected these countries
   and which one you deferred; a graph or indicator that changed your entry
   decision; why digital exporting fits your venture; one cross-cultural
   difference affecting product or communication design; a founder test of
   the same marketing message shown in two cultural contexts; and the one
   AI-generated cultural generalization from Task 3 above that you had to
   verify, qualify, or reject.
